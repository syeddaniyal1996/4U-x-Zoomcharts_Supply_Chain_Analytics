# Project Overview:
This repository features a high-performance Supply Chain Analytics Suite designed for 4U Foods. The project transforms raw operational data into a strategic command center, providing a 360-degree view of commercial growth, supplier efficiency, and inventory health.  The dashboard is built using a "Midnight Professional" theme, optimized for executive clarity and deep-dive technical analysis.

# 🛠 Core Analytical Modules

# 1. Commercial Overview:

i. Revenue & Margin Analysis: 
This module tracks the financial heartbeat of the supply chain, focusing on profitability and market penetration.

ii. Financial Performance: 
Monitored $605.6K in Total Revenue with a solid 34.4% Gross Margin.

iii. Top Revenue Drivers: 
SnowCup Ice Cream SKU 3 emerged as the leading product, generating $27.7K. 

iv. Channel Performance: 
Identified Retail as the primary sales engine ($253.0K), followed by HoReCa and Distributors.

v. Geographic Insights: 
East Europe stands as the strongest region, contributing 29.49% of the total revenue share.

# 2. Supply & Suppliers: 

i. Risk & Delivery Performance:
Focused on upstream resilience, this section evaluates vendor reliability and potential points of failure. 

ii. Fulfillment Excellence: 
Maintained a 92.5% Supplier OTIF (On-Time In-Full) rate. 

iii. Quality Control: 
Actively tracked a 2.8% Defect Rate and an average delay of only 0.1 days per order.

iv. Risk Stratification: 
Utilized a Revenue by Risk Tier model to segment the $605.6K spend into Low, Medium, and High-risk categories for better contingency planning.

# 3. Inventory & Quality: 

i. Stock Health & Waste Analysis:
This module addresses operational inefficiencies and product loss mitigation. 

ii. Waste Management: 
Identified 8,177 Expired Units, with significant loss clusters in the Bakery and Beverage categories.

iii. Stock Continuity: 
Analyzed 75 Total Stockout Incidents to identify patterns in failed orders.

iv. Hub Efficiency: 
Comparative analysis of accuracy and quality issue rates across major distribution centers like the Warsaw Hub, Berlin Export Hub, and Tallinn Cross-Dock.

i. Advanced Visualizations: 
Leveraged custom SVG-driven KPIs for instant performance snapshots of Stockout Rates and OTIF trends.
